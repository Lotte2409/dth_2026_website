# Open source

## Omeka

**What is Omeka?**
Omeka is an Open Source software, that helps you create an online archive. According to their [Github](https://github.com/omeka/Omeka) they provide “A flexible web publishing platform for the display of library, museum and scholarly collections, archives and exhibitions”  It basically creates a searchable online data base. Omeka was created by the *Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media* in 2007.

### How would it help in my Study?
As Omeka is an open software tool meant to create digital archives, it can be helpful to sort my data both when it comes to the historical aspects of European Studies. Especially, for the MA thesis, as I am going to look at Germany's history, it is going to be beneficial to have an online archive of the useful data. As it archives digital images, text, and other multi-media formats it creates a good foundation of analyzing social media posts, which is also relevant for the thesis as we are going to look at how social media is deployed to target the youth. It basically creates the foundation of data collection.
